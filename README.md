### Current & recent research
| Project | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [**Tumour-type-conditioned brain tumour segmentation**](https://github.com/farzanasupti/Brain-Tumor-Segmentation) | U-Net segmentation on T1-CE MRI whose decoder is steered by a predicted tumour-type posterior (FiLM), evaluated patient-disjoint with cross-validation; includes an analysis of how slice-level leakage inflates reported scores | Ongoing |
| [**Ensemble deep learning for skin disease classification**](https://github.com/farzanasupti/skin-disease-ensemble-2026) | A custom CNN and seven ImageNet-pretrained models compared on five-class skin-condition classification, with image-level and patient-level evaluation | First author, under review at *Scientific Reports* |
| [**Duplicate-aware skin disease classification**](https://github.com/farzanasupti/skin-disease-classification) | Eight models on the public Mendeley skin dataset, which turned out to be 341 original photos with 27 augmented copies each; grouped cross-validation, scoring on original photos only, and MC-Dropout for referring low-confidence cases | Manuscript in preparation |
| **PP-SAM replication** (CVPRW 2024) | Few-shot SAM adaptation with perturbed box prompts on Kvasir-SEG, adapted for limited GPU memory | Completed |
| **U-Mamba for brain tumour segmentation** | U-Net with a custom VisionMamba block for 2D BraTS slices | Ongoing |
| **River erosion analysis**, NSU Civil & Environmental Eng. | CNN / transfer learning on satellite imagery | 2024–2025 |
